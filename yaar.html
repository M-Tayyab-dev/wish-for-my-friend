Zeera,

Salgirah mubarak, yaar! Tumhare saath guzra har din ek acchi photo jaisa hai, thoda unplanned, thoda dhoop wala, aur hamesha yaad rehne wala.

Allah tumhein sehat, kamyabi aur sukoon de. Tumhara shauq tumhein bohat aage le jaye, aur tumhare lens ko hamesha naye nazare milte rahein.

Chalo ab cake kaatte hain, phir ek group photo. Tum photo khainchna, hum pose denge.

Tumhara yaar
